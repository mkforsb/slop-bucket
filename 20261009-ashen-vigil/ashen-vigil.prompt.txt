❯ Let's create a PS1-style demake of Dark Souls, starting with the Undead Asylum area and the Asylum Demon boss fight.

  We'll use Rust and Dioxus targetting a web build, WebGL, WebAudio.

  We'll initially have only a basic knight character with a medium-sized sword and shield, but try to keep in mind that we'll add lots more stuff later, so design
  for that. We'll implement the full basic moveset with light and heavy attacks (and chains), block and dodge.

  Feel free to:
  - Install anything you need.
  - WebSearch anything you need, e.g about Dark Souls, perhaps a walkthrough of the Undead Asylum, PS1/PSX demakes, PSX texture aesthetics.
  - Download one or more YouTube videos from playthroughs of the Undead Asylum and Asylum Demon boss fight.
  - Change names and terms to avoid copyright infringement.
