● I've built Purrfect Pals — Caring for your pet cat. It's one self-contained file: dist/purrfect-pals.html (646 KB, Three.js/WebGL). Open it in a browser; it needs
  no server or network, and progress saves to localStorage.

  I played it through end to end in headless Chromium. Adoption, all five care scenes, customization, the menu, and save → reload → continue all work with no       
  console errors, and I checked a phone-sized screen too. I couldn't listen to any audio. The meows, purring and sound effects are all synthesized in code. I       
  rendered every meow style offline and checked the signal: none are silent and none clip. How they actually sound is untested, so that's the thing to check first. 

  The cats are built from simple shapes, with an outline for a cartoon look. The coat pattern is painted by a shader, so stripes and patches flow smoothly across   
  the body, head, legs and tail. They blink, twitch their ears, blush, make happy ^^ eyes, and look at whatever you're doing.

  Scenes
  - Adopt: you start with a random shelter kitty and customize it on a turntable. There are 8 coat patterns (solid, tabby, tuxedo, calico, tortie, pointed, spotted,
    van) with colours and white markings. You can also change the eyes (including odd eyes), nose, head, ears, body build, legs, tail, fluffiness, and collar/bell. 
    For the meow there are 7 styles plus pitch, length and raspiness sliders, with a preview. The ✂️ button lets you restyle the cat later.
  - Home: the cat roams on its own. It naps, watches the window, eats if food is out, uses the litter box, gets the zoomies, and meows with a thought bubble when a 
    need is low.
  - Feed: pour kibble from the bag (it can overflow the bowl), spoon out wet food, toss treats, or refill the water. Eating too many treats gets a gentle warning,  
    and a full cat turns its nose up.
  - Pet: stroke with a hand or a brush. Cheeks and chin are the favourites; the tail and face are not. Petting brings purring and kneading. Too much rough handling 
    earns a swat. A very content cat rolls over to show its belly, which is a trap (bunny kicks).
  - Play:
    - Feather wand: the cat stalks, wiggles its butt, then pounces.
    - Laser pointer: an endless chase with no catch, plus a tip about ending on a real toy.
    - Yarn ball: throw it and the cat bats it around.
  - Litter: scoop clumps and drag them into the bin, top up or change the litter, or tap "Nature calls" to watch the cat use the box. It refuses a dirty box.       

  Four needs (food, love, fun, litter) slowly drop over time and add up to the cat's mood face.

  To rebuild after editing: python3 build.py. It uses a standalone esbuild and a copy of three.js stored in the project, so Node isn't needed. The README covers the
  file layout, and dev/ holds the screenshot test helpers I used.
