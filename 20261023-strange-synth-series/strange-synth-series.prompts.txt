    Let's build a very strange synth. Rust/Dioxus focusing on a webapp target, WebAudio.

      WebSearch for strange synth ideas.

      Requirements:
      - At least 30 strange parameters
      - At least 50 factory presets
      - A button \"RND\" that mutates every parameter except for locked ones
      - Shift-click knobs/widgets to lock their parameter
      - A master filter (12/24dB Moog, bandpass, hipass), EQ (lowcut, lo, mid, hi, hicut), and end-of-chain reverb (Dattorro algo)
      - Cool visuals

      Wishlist of factory presets, the names of which can inspire the synth:
      - Metal-on-metal screeching industrial elevator
      - Struck bowed metal sheet
      - UFO engine room
      - T-rex Roar
      - Godzilla Laser Beam
      - Resonating metal clothes rack
      - Hitting muted strings with a plastic pipe
      - Haunting singing glass
      - Thoughts of the machine


    You're free to install anything you need in this sandbox. Keep all outputs in this folder. Small logical commits is encouraged.
