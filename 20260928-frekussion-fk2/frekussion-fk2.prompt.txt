First grab this public repo: https://github.com/mkforsb/synkussion-sk1-slop

Let's build a new synthesizer similar to the Synkussion SK-1. What I have in mind is the same focus on percussive sounds/envelopes, the same dual-channel setup with per-channel reverb, but with a complete focus on FM, including modulation between the two channels. Each channel should also be a fairly capable FM voice by itself, possibly each with a dedicated separate noise oscillator. We're looking for all types of percussive sounds. Everything from traditional drum sounds to metallic clangs and discordant and hollow hits and stabs.

For inspiration, WebSearch for things like "FM percussive sounds", "FM percussion synthesis", "Elektron model cycles"

I'll let you pick a name for the new synth :)

We'll stick to Rust/Dioxus and keep the same dual build targets (desktop and web)

Have fun!
