First grab the public repo https://github.com/mkforsb/stabkussion-st1-slop

I'd like us to build the successor, Stabkussion ST-2.

The premise is simply "more is better". Our objective will be to double the amount of synthesis-related
knobs in every engine by adding meaningful features that a user would want to control, and to add a new
cross-modulation panel that enables various weird and wonderful ways for the two synths to modulate and
circuit-bend each other. We'll keep all the randomization features and parameter locking.

WebSearch is strongly encouraged, inspiration could perhaps be found in modern synths like Serum and Vital,
or more experimental stuff in stuff from Erica Synths and similar.

Keep all files in our current workdir, don't write directly to /mnt

Try to have fun! Go!
