First grab this public repo: https://github.com/mkforsb/synkussion-sk1-slop

Let's build a new synthesizer similar to the Synkussion SK-1. What I have in mind is the same focus on percussive sounds/envelopes, the same dual-channel setup with per-channel reverb, but to go completely wild with the oscillator/mode designs and reach for very experimental stuff. Let's consider things like comb filter resonators, physically-based string models, granular, random wavetables, etc.

For inspiration, WebSearch for things like "experimental synths", "experimental modular synth percussion" and perhaps something on "unique features of vital and serum"

I'll let you pick a name for the new synth :)

We'll stick to Rust/Dioxus and keep the same dual build targets (desktop and web)

Have fun!
