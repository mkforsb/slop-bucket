Let's build a techno session groovebox. Rust/Dioxus, build targets web (WebAudio) and desktop (PulseAudio)

Chainnels:
- Kick synth, pitch, decay, sub, sub decay, drum hit harmonics (non-tonal), distortion, rumble reverb (ducked by the kick itself) with its own strong eq
- Closed hihat synth, pitch, attack, decay
- Open hihat
- Ride hihat
- Snare
- Clap
- Low tom
- Mid tom
- High tom
- Low conga
- Mid conga
- High conga
- FM stab synth - three-voice chord

All channels have synth-params randomization button, solo, mute, lo/mid/hi EQ and sends to a master tempo-synced delay and a master reverb

Master DJ filter (single knob lowpass-bandpass-highpass sweep) similar to SuperFilter on the MC101/MC707

Transport:
- Play/pause
- BPM
- Swing

Per-channel step sequencer:
- Similar to Roland TR gear (row of 16 buttons shared by all channels, pick channel first then use row of buttons)
- Ability to go up to 128 steps like the MC101/MC707, i.e. 1-8 pages of 16 steps
- Per-step velocity/amplitude, ability to draw curve
- Per-step pitch adjustment
- Per-step probability
- Polyrhythm, i.e. selecting any step lower than the step length to be the loop point (looping back to start)
- Copy/paste functionality to e.g. rapidly duplicate 16 steps into 32 and so on
- Three variations A/B/C, each holding its own 16-128 step sequence, cued and tempo-synced when switching (switch when currently playing step sequence would have looped)
- Randomization features

Master effects:
- As mentioned above: master delay, master reverb, DJ filter
- Sidechain compressor ("ducker") automatically wired to use the kick as control signal to duck everything else
- Limiter with makeup gain

You pick the name. Have fun!
