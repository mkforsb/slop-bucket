❯ First grab the public repo https://github.com/mkforsb/xenokussion-xk1-slop

  Taking inspiration from the tech, look, and feel of Xenokussion (Rust/Dioxus, Web/WebAudio, Desktop/PulseAudio),

  I want you to create something COMPLETELY DIFFERENT. I want to be SURPRISED AND SHOCKED. Atleast every 3rd sound
  should make me go WHAT. WHAT IS THIS. WHO WOULD EVEN THINK OF SOMETHING LIKE THIS. It should do this while also
  making sounds that aren't entirely unpleasant to listen to. Aim for "bewilderingly interesting" rather than
  "just horrific noise". At almost every step of implementing any synthesis you should stop and think "could I do
  this in a more unusual way?"

  Sounds do not need to be percussive, but some could be.

  Keep the randomization features, but add parameter locking for every mutatable parameter, enabled by Ctrl-clicking the knob/slider/button/widget.

  Keep the master reverbs.

  I'll leave the name of the synth to you. Go now. Be brave. And don't forget to have fun.
