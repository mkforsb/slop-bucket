First grab the public repo https://github.com/mkforsb/xenokussion-xk1-slop

Let's build a new synthesizer heavily based on Xenokussion XK-1, but quite a different machine.

We're sticking to Rust/Dioxus and the dual target of Web and Desktop.

This time, rather than percussive sounds, we're looking for ambient atmosphere drone sounds.
I'm thinking we essentially take an XK-1 but without the UI, we turn up both MUTATE knobs to max,
and simulate hitting Space. We sample (render) the output instead of playing it, using signal
strength to detect the end of the current sound. Then we apply something very similar to PaulStretch
to stretch and smear the sound in an extreme fashion.

I'm thinking the new UI could have a couple of controls to influence the underlying XK-1, such as
setting the amount of MUTATE to be applied (maximum by default), picking which engines should be active
(available for the random choice applied by MUTATE, all engines by default), and perhaps a control to
influence the pitch. Maybe more if you can come up with some.

Most importantly, the new UI should have the following three controls:

- Play/stop: starts and stops the drone machine/synth
- Hit frequency / pattern: slider to control how often a new XK-1 hit is generated (essentially a
  simulated press of Space) and rendered and eventually mixed into the PaulStretch output stream,
  perhaps with different settings for intervals, such as Euclidean.
- Stretch factor: the amount of stretching applied in the PaulStretch algorithm, similar to the
  original program where you could pick e.g. "50.0x" to stretch a 1-second sound to 50 seconds.

Finally, the final stretched output should go into it's own dedicated effects section where we'll
have another Dattorro reverb, this time also with a shimmer setting, and we'll also have a spacey
ping-pong stereo delay with feedback and interesting damping modes (e.g. band pass filter on each
feedback) and perhaps also some interesting intervals beyond the regular N milliseconds.
Both the reverb and feedback should be able to be turned completely off.

The synth should be able to figure out how many hits to keep pre-rendered in order to be able to
feed the PaulStretch output stream according to the current parameters.

For inspiration, WebSearch for "paulstretch", "drone synths", "techno atmosphere drones", "ambient
techno drones", "shimmer reverb", "space echo".

Clearly experimental stuff, so have fun with it! And pick a cool name!
